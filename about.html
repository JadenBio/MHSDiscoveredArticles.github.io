About the Unknown

The so called "cult of maui high" is a group formed of workers from Maui High School. It was assumed to be made in 1967 and is based on the forbidden ISRAELI texts.

The Story

The cult takes place in campus of maui high school. Maui High has a long history of terrible events that were never noticed until now. After a frightening incident everything changed, a group of teenagers discovers a possible connection between recent attacks and a curse from the past.

Assumed Members of the Cult

  • Tilerio Odagata: The main leader of the cult.
  • Keary Wansona: Tilerios's right hand man.
  • Pearston Rogderizo: The cult's messenger.
  • Lake Anitamaxwin: One of Tilerio's advisers.

The Town of Kahului

Kahului is an important part of the cult because its history has an interesting past. The town's reputation hides something, a story much darker.

In Kahului, the past is never truly buried.
